## Maqsad (North Star)

1. **Sotuvlarni x4 ga oshirish** (hozir sotuvlar yaxshi, lekin 4 barobar o'sish kerak).
2. **Xalqaro exponentlar** — nafaqat O'zbekistondan, balki Turkiya, Xitoy, Rossiya, Eron,
   Hindiston va Yevropadan kompaniyalar jalb qilish.
3. **AI-agentlar konveyeri** — marketing jarayonining deyarli barchasi (lead topish, isitish,
   outreach, kontent, follow-up, CRM) avtomatlashtirilgan bo'lishi.

## x4 formulasi (dekompozitsiya)

Sotuv o'sishi 4 ta richagning ko'paytmasi:

```
Sotuv = Lead soni × Konversiya × O'rtacha chek × Qaytish (retention)
```

| Richag | Hozir (taxminiy) | Maqsad | Qanday |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lead soni** | Baza | ×2 | Xalqaro kanallar (Alibaba, elchixona, diaspora), raqib baza, target ads |
| **Konversiya** | Baza | ×1.5 | Isitish voronkasi, shaxsiy AI-video, FOMO/joy xaritasi, buyer dasturi |
| **O'rtacha chek** | Baza | ×1.3 | Paketlash (Start/Business/Premium), All-Inclusive, burchak narxi |
| **Retention** | Baza | ×1.4 | Qayta sotish, elchi dasturi, yopiq klub, umrbod a'zolik |

> ×2 × 1.5 × 1.3 × 1.4 ≈ **x5.4** — ya'ni x4 realistik, agar 4 richag birga ishlasa.
